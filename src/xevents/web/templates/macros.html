Why it looks different

This is what a veteran — or whoever is helping them — receives. It drops the operator chrome (provenance, panel arithmetic, statuses), keeps the reviewed sentences at 16.5px and prints on one page in black on white.

Nothing is paraphrased: the sentences, the safety line and the escalation signs are the card's own text, in the card's own order.

Medical alerts for extreme weather

Heatwaves, hurricanes, wildfire smoke and power outages are far more dangerous for some patients, because of their condition or the medicines they take. For example, someone on dialysis can't miss treatment when the power goes out, and some heart and psychiatric medicines make heat much harder to tolerate.

This prototype watches public weather, air-quality and outage data. When an event hits an area, it estimates how many at-risk patients each nearby hospital serves and issues an alert card: practical steps for the care team, and plain-language guidance for patients and caregivers.

Good to know

Not for clinical use. This demo uses only public, aggregate data and no patient records. Patient counts are estimates, and each one shows how it was calculated. The first profile uses U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs hospitals. Other health systems can be added as profiles.
